Our Story

Started on April 2nd, 1989 by Lester D’souza

Lester & Monisha D’souza

Best Restaurant in Ahmedabad. Upper Crust’s name takes inspiration from the oven-baked products having a crispy upper layer. Started on April 2nd, 1989 by Lester D’souza, Café Upper Crust has grown to become Ahmedabad's most iconic culinary destination.
