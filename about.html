Our Story

Café Upper Crust was founded in 1989 by Lester D'Souza with a vision to bring world-class bakery and dining experience to Ahmedabad. What started as a small bakery has now grown into one of the most recognised restaurant and bakery chains in the city.

With over three decades of culinary excellence, Upper Crust has been serving the people of Ahmedabad with a diverse menu that spans across Indian, Italian, Chinese, and Thai cuisines. Our bakery section offers fresh breads, cookies, pastries, cakes, and an array of baked delicacies prepared daily.

Today, Café Upper Crust operates 8 outlets across Ahmedabad, each maintaining the same standard of quality, taste, and hospitality that our founder envisioned over 35 years ago.

Lester D'Souza

Founder & Visionary

With a passion for baking and a dream to create Ahmedabad's finest dining destination, Lester D'Souza established Café Upper Crust in 1989. His dedication to quality ingredients, authentic recipes, and warm hospitality has been the cornerstone of the Upper Crust legacy.

Our Journey
