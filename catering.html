Shagun Catering by Upper Crust

Shagun Catering, a venture by the Upper Crust Group, brings the same quality and taste that has made Café Upper Crust a household name in Ahmedabad. We offer comprehensive catering services for all types of events — from intimate family gatherings to grand weddings and corporate conferences.

With over 35 years of culinary expertise, our team of experienced chefs prepares a wide range of cuisines including Indian, Continental, Chinese, Thai, and Italian. We customise every menu to match your preferences, dietary requirements, and event theme.
